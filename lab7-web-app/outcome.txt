This lab advanced my first customized learning outcome, to analyze and improve a technical investment system by automating repetitive steps, improving workflow efficiency, and keeping the analysis accurate and reliable. I mapped a real workflow from my work as an analyst at the Scholars Opportunity Fund, a systematic hedge fund, and found where information gets lost between a research idea and a usable result. Modeling the process by its inputs, activities, outputs, and customers, then redesigning it across three drafts, is the skill that connects most directly to the outcome. Adding validation, a shared queue, and a dashboard showed me how to remove manual follow-up without losing control of the data. The lab also touched my second outcome, because I had to test every AI-assisted feature and keep fund data out of the sample content. In my career in asset management, I expect to build and improve research and testing workflows like this one, and measuring turnaround time will show whether an improvement actually saves time.
