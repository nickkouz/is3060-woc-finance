About the Process

The research request process is how an analyst gets help from the fund on a specific research task, such as testing a catalyst signal, pulling filing data, or reviewing a corporate event. The fund is a systematic hedge fund that mainly finds its catalysts through event-driven work, and its strategies range well beyond any single event type. Research needs to be timely and consistent.

Process Users

  • Analysts who request research
  • Pod leads who assign and review it
  • Portfolio managers who use the output
  • Faculty advisor, who oversees the fund

Inputs and Resources

  • Message or email describing the idea or data need
  • SEC filings (EDGAR), market data, and event feeds
  • Shared tracking spreadsheet
  • Analyst and pod lead time

Activities

  1. Analyst messages a pod lead
  2. Pod lead replies when available
  3. Request is added to the spreadsheet
  4. Someone researches and posts a memo

Outputs

A short research memo, dataset, or backtest result that shows whether a catalyst or signal is worth adding to a strategy.

Customers

The portfolio managers and the fund as a whole, who rely on that research to decide which signals and strategies to run.

Current Information

Update (Draft 3): Requests are submitted with the form, tracked through five workflow stages, and summarized on the dashboard. Original process information is kept for reference.
  • How to ask: Message your pod lead with the ticker or universe and a short description of the idea.
  • How to check status: Open the shared tracking spreadsheet and look for your row, if one was added.
  • Typical wait: Varies. Requests that arrive during exam weeks can wait several days.
  • Known issue: Two analysts sometimes research the same name or signal without knowing it.
